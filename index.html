Rutherford Scatter

Fire particles at a hidden structure, read how they scatter, then place the shapes you deduce. Pick a level:

How to play

1
Fire. Move the cannon around the ring and aim inward. Most particles pass straight through — space is mostly empty.
2
Read the scatter. A particle that hits the hidden structure kicks off at an angle, leaving an orange spark at the point of impact. Cluster enough sparks and the surfaces appear.
3
Place. Drag the right shapes from the tray into the ring and rotate them to match your sparks. Some levels offer decoy shapes — placing a wrong one scores nothing. Fewer shots and a closer match score higher.
