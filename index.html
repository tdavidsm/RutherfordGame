Rutherford Scatter

A hidden structure sits at the center — you can't see it. Fire particles from the ring and watch how they scatter. From the pattern, deduce the shapes and place them.

How to play

1
Fire. Move the cannon around the ring and aim inward. Most particles pass straight through — space is mostly empty.
2
Read the scatter. A particle that hits the hidden structure kicks off at an angle, leaving an orange spark at the point of impact. Cluster enough sparks and the surfaces appear.
3
Place. Drag the real shapes onto where you believe they sit and rotate them to match. Reveal to see how close you got.
